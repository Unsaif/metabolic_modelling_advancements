# Evidence brief 04 — Clinical and rare-disease applications (state as of September 2026)

Compiled 4 Sept 2026 by Claude research agents for the "Metabolic modelling improvements" project. ~25 searches and ~50 primary-source fetches; PubMed/PMC/ScienceDirect/Cell.com blocked some fetches, so a few items rest on metadata/abstracts and are marked as such. Items marked *unverified* should be checked before being cited. This brief feeds the Landscape & Roadmap document (00-landscape-and-roadmap.md).

## (A) Key developments 2023–2026

- **Infant WBMs (Zaunseder et al., Cell Metab 2024).** 360 sex-specific, organ-resolved (26 organs, 6 cell types, >80k reactions) models for days 1–180; 10,000 newborn models parameterised from >2M newborn-screening (NBS) profiles (17 amino acids + 12 acylcarnitines, birth weight); growth tracked within WHO quartiles; covers 595/1,364 IMD genes (636 IMDs); 5 IMDs (PKU, IVA, GA1, Gaucher, kynureninase deficiency) simulated with biomarker direction consistent with IEMbase across 3 timepoints; dietary treatment responses simulated. [Cell Metab](https://www.cell.com/cell-metabolism/fulltext/S1550-4131(24)00182-7) · [bioRxiv full text](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.10.20.563364v1.full)
- **Dynamic infant WBM–PBPK coupling (Zaunseder et al., bioRxiv Nov 2024).** Iterative ODE–FBA coupling to predict biomarker *concentrations* over time; IVA C5 predicted 3.5 µmol/L at 72 h vs literature medians 2.6 (attenuated)/10.6 (classic); authors state quantitative predictions underestimate patients and need clinical validation. [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.11.25.625291v1)
- **PKU × infant microbiome WBMs (Moghimi et al., Microbiome 2026).** 90 healthy-infant metagenomes in germ-free vs colonised infant WBMs; microbes raise predicted tyrosine availability but ~23% of models stay tyrosine-low (Bifidobacterium/Escherichia-dependent). No patient validation. [Microbiome](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40168-026-02511-9)
- **Rare heterozygous variants ≈ "graded IEMs" (Scherer et al., Nat Genet 2025).** 4,737 GCKD participants, exome + plasma/urine metabolomics, 235 gene–metabolite associations; WBM "virtual IEM" knockouts for 24 genes predicted association direction at 73.3% (Harvey) / 76.7% (Harvetta). [Nat Genet](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-024-01965-7)
- **Recon4IMD (Horizon Europe, €7.87M, Jun 2023–May 2027, Galway-coordinated).** ~1,945 diagnosed + 685 undiagnosed IMD patients; PROTRIDER, ReconX knowledge graph, biomarkers across 28 IMDs; explicit goal of regulatory-grade decision-support software. [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101080997/reporting/de) · [recon4imd.org](https://recon4imd.org/) · [publications](https://recon4imd.org/publications/)
- **Gaucher disease CBM (Liu et al., Orphanet J Rare Dis 2026).** Recon3D → macrophage models (XomicsToModel), 150 paired GD/control models from two transcriptomic datasets + exometabolomics; predicts OXPHOS→glycolysis shift, ceramide, cholesterol dysregulation; ASAH1/CPT1A modifiers; "several predictions corroborated by independent findings". [OJRD](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13023-026-04206-8)
- **Sampling-based biomarker prediction (Cooke et al., PLoS Comput Biol 2024, SAMBA).** Human1 flux sampling with z-scores; 80% accuracy on 49 IEMs at rank cutoff 110 vs 76.8% for the FVA (Shlomi-style) method. [PLoS CB](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011381)
- **Tissue-resolved IEM FBA (Liu & Westerhoff, JIMD 2023).** Competitive BBB transport + 3 compartments explains PKU brain pathology and diet rationale; qualitative only. [JIMD](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jimd.12603)
- **Microbiome–WBM disease studies:** PD, 435 PD/219 HC metagenomes vs 116 replicated blood metabolites → reduced leucine/butyrate/pantothenate/nicotinic-acid capacity (Hensen & Thiele, Gut Microbes 2025) [link](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19490976.2025.2554195); AD, 383 metagenomes → predicted ↑SAM, arginine, creatine, taurine, formate (bioRxiv Oct 2025) [link](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.28.685084v1); 1,065 ageing individuals, predictions corroborated by serum metabolomics (deoxycholate/APOE) (Gut Microbes Rep 2024) [link](https://doi.org/10.1080/29933935.2024.2443171); AD urine formate (Sci Rep 2024) [link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-55960-3).
- **Host–microbiome GEMs in IBD (Taubenheim et al., Nat Commun 2025).** 62 patients, 296 biopsy/324 blood/565 16S samples, 150 metabolomes; 16/18 serum-significant metabolites map to model-enriched host subsystems. [Nat Commun](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-60233-2)
- **LLM-curated human GEM: Human2 (Luo et al., PNAS, 8 Apr 2026).** LLM + automated GitHub checks for curation; tissue/organ models across demographic groups; enzyme-constrained *dynamic whole-body* framework for feeding→fasting interorgan exchange. Human-GEM v2.0.0 (12,931 rxns; 8,461 mets; 2,848 genes). [PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2516511123) · [GitHub](https://github.com/SysBioChalmers/Human-GEM)
- **Nutrition/diet applications.** 12 diets × Harvey/Harvetta for metabolic-syndrome markers, no cohort validation (Alessi et al., Front Physiol 2025) [link](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2025.1586750/full); WBM precision-nutrition prospectus (Nap, Thinnes & Thiele, Proc Nutr Soc 2026) [link](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-nutrition-society/article/wholebody-metabolic-modelling-and-its-prospects-in-precision-nutrition/DCEBB09123BD1AE96295043798B30D32).
- **ERC PoC "iChatRD" (Jun 2026, Thiele & Thinnes, €150k/18 mo).** AI-assisted CDSS coupling metabolic modelling with NLP to suggest expert-level IMD diagnoses from patient descriptions; the award is named iChatRD and builds on the ChatRD prototype (the university news item refers to ChatRD/ChatRD2.0 as the prototype) — the first explicit GEM+LLM clinical-decision-support effort found in this research (the "first" is this brief's inference, not a claim in the source). [Galway news](https://www.universityofgalway.ie/about-us/news-and-events/news-archive/2026/june/university-of-galway-researchers-secure-european-research-council-awards-1.html)

## (B) Validated clinically vs. in-silico only

**Clinically/experimentally anchored (still retrospective, no prospective trial):**
- WBM IEM biomarker directions: 215/252 (85.3%, Harvetta) and 214/252 (84.9%, Harvey) biofluid-specific biomarkers across **57 IEMs**, vs 103/205 (50.2%) for Recon3D; BMR explains 61.3% of variance in independent data (beats Mifflin-St Jeor R²=0.49). [MSB 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.15252/msb.20198982)
- Recon 2: 49 IEMs at **77% accuracy** vs experimental data. [Nat Biotechnol 2013](https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt.2488)
- WBM virtual-IEM directions vs population metabolomics: 73–77% (24 genes). [Nat Genet 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-024-01965-7)
- Infant model growth within WHO quartiles; biomarker directions match IEMbase. [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.10.20.563364v1.full)
- IBD: model-predicted host subsystems agree with 16/18 measured serum metabolites. [Nat Commun 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-60233-2)
- Ageing/AD microbiome predictions corroborated in same-individual serum metabolomics. [Gut Microbes Rep 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/29933935.2024.2443171)

**In-silico only / re-parameterisation without validation:** 12-diet metabolic-syndrome WBM study (authors: single average subjects, predictions "untested", no insulin) [link](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2025.1586750/full); PKU tyrosine/microbiome (no patient data) [link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40168-026-02511-9); AD microbiome WBM preprint (no metabolomic confirmation) [link](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.28.685084v1); PD WBM markers linked to taxa, not measured metabolites [link](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19490976.2025.2554195); dynamic infant PBPK-WBM (qualitative literature comparison only) [link](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.11.25.625291v1). **No GEM/WBM tool is in clinical use or has published prospective clinical evaluation**; Recon4IMD is the only programme explicitly building toward regulated software [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101080997/reporting/de).

## (C) Limitations, critiques, failure modes

- **Shlomi 2009 accuracy degrades on real clinical data:** RBC model precision 0.73/recall 0.40; manual amino-acid disorders 0.76/0.56; **clinical database 0.41 precision, 0.10 recall** — the canonical warning that curated-set performance overstates clinical performance. Steady state, no regulation, missing transporters cause false predictions. [MSB 2009](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/msb.2009.22)
- **Direction-only, not magnitude.** WBM/IEMbase comparisons are qualitative (↑/↓); the dynamic model underestimates absolute C5. [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.11.25.625291v1)
- **Single-compartment blindness.** SAMBA authors: whole-body-as-one-system oversimplification cannot detect tissue-specific compensation; low-ranked metabolites have near-identical z-scores. [PLoS CB 2024](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011381)
- **Prediction–measurement disconnect.** AD urine formate: microbial secretion predicted lower, but no significant WBM difference; measured formate non-significant after FDR. [Sci Rep 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-55960-3)
- **Missing biology:** no gut microbiome in infant WBMs, constant milk composition, no protein turnover, no formula diets [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.10.20.563364v1.full); no insulin/endocrine control in Harvey/Harvetta [Front Physiol 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2025.1586750/full); enzyme kinetics/regulation largely absent, food databases miss phytochemicals; authors state predictions "represent hypotheses… must be validated" [Proc Nutr Soc 2026](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-nutrition-society/article/wholebody-metabolic-modelling-and-its-prospects-in-precision-nutrition/DCEBB09123BD1AE96295043798B30D32).
- **Method-choice sensitivity.** Extraction-method choice materially changes context-specific model predictions; no universally best method [Opdam et al., Cell Systems 2017](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.cels.2017.01.010); transcriptomic-integration benchmarking shows tight condition clustering can coexist with bad growth prediction [Front Bioinform 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fbinf.2026.1715377/full).
- **Microbiome model caveats:** compositional data, steady state, no community dynamics, taxa absent from AGORA2 discarded, medications/lifestyle ignored. [Sci Rep 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-55960-3)
- **Cell-type mismatch in rare disease:** GD modelling limited by fibroblast-dominated patient material vs macrophage pathology. [OJRD 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13023-026-04206-8)

## (D) AI/LLM at this intersection

- **Human2 / LLM-assisted GEM curation (PNAS, Apr 2026).** LLMs + automated GitHub checks; tissue/demographic models; enzyme-constrained dynamic whole-body simulation. Abstract/metadata report **no accuracy metrics** — evaluation detail unverified from the full text in this brief (see brief 01 for figures obtained from a separate fetch). [PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2516511123) · [GitHub](https://github.com/SysBioChalmers/Human-GEM)
- **LLM rare-disease diagnosis meta-analysis (medRxiv, Mar 2026).** 15 studies, 19 system–dataset entries, 39,529 cases; pooled **recall@1 = 43.3% (95% CI 35.1–51.6)**; augmented (agentic/RAG/fine-tuned) 52.5% vs standalone 35.4% (p=0.004); RareBench 52.0% vs Phenopacket Store 21.7% (p<0.001); **all 19 entries high risk of bias (data leakage), no prospective validation**, I²=99.6%. IMDs not analysed separately. [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.26.26349194v1.full.pdf)
- **LLMs on NBS tandem-MS data (Weng et al., ICIC 2025).** Qwen2.5-7B, Gemma-7B, Llama3-8B, Medical-Llama3-8B, MedAlpaca-7B; ~3,000 newborns, 114 confirmed cases, five amino-acid disorders; LoRA+quantisation; higher recall than classical ML with false-positive rate <2%. No mechanistic model coupling. [Springer LNCS](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-981-95-0036-9_8.pdf)
- **ERC PoC ChatRD (2026)** — GEM + NLP CDSS for IMD; no published evaluation yet. [Galway](https://www.universityofgalway.ie/about-us/news-and-events/news-archive/2026/june/university-of-galway-researchers-secure-european-research-council-awards-1.html)
- Adjacent: MACAW semi-automatic GEM error detection ([Genome Biol 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-025-03533-6)); KinForm kcat/Km prediction ([npj Syst Biol Appl 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41540-026-00692-5)). A Crossref sweep found **no** published LLM-agent-drives-FBA system for clinical use.

## (E) Opportunities for frontier-model intelligence

1. **Build the missing IEM ground-truth benchmark.** IEMbase (2,048 IEMs, Aug 2026, no documented bulk download/API [iembase.org](https://www.iembase.org/)), HPO, Orphadata (CC-BY-4.0 [orphadata.com](https://www.orphadata.com/)) and HMDB 5.0 (23 biospecimens, normal/abnormal ranges, CC-BY-NC [hmdb.ca](https://hmdb.ca/about)) are not harmonised into disease→(metabolite, biofluid, direction, magnitude, age) tuples. *Success:* a versioned, licence-clean benchmark of ≥1,000 IEM–biomarker–biofluid triples with age/sex strata, reproducing the 85.3%/84.9% WBM figures and yielding per-disease precision/recall.
2. **Move from direction to magnitude, scored properly.** Current claims are ↑/↓ agreement; clinical utility needs concentration intervals. *Success:* calibrated prediction intervals for ≥20 IEMs benchmarked against NBS/diagnostic ranges, with coverage ≥80% and reported false-positive rate at NBS cutoffs.
3. **Automated, auditable curation of the ~40% of IMD genes absent from WBMs** (595/1,364 covered). *Success:* +300 IMD genes with reactions, transporters and compartments, each with literature citation and passing MACAW-style error checks; measurable lift in biomarker recall.
4. **Fix the transporter/compartment failure mode that Shlomi flagged in 2009 and remains unsolved.** *Success:* LLM-extracted transporter–substrate–tissue evidence added to Recon3D/Human2, raising clinical-database recall above 0.10 on a held-out patient set.
5. **Method-choice as a learned decision, not a coin flip** (Opdam 2017; Front Bioinform 2026). *Success:* a selector that picks extraction/integration method + parameters per dataset and beats every fixed method on a multi-tissue held-out validation set.
6. **Mechanistic + LLM hybrid diagnosis, prospectively evaluated.** Standalone LLM rare-disease recall@1 is 35.4%, augmented 52.5%, all high-bias/leakage. Recon4IMD's 685 undiagnosed patients are the natural test set. *Success:* WBM-derived candidate-metabolite priors added to an LLM agent give ≥10-point recall@1 gain on a leakage-controlled, prospectively collected IMD cohort.
7. **Regulatory-grade evidence packaging.** Software qualifies as a device under EU MDR Art. 2(1) when intended for a medical purpose ([EUR-Lex 2017/745](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32017R0745)); Recon4IMD explicitly targets this. *Success:* a reproducible, version-pinned validation dossier (dataset provenance, per-disease performance, failure modes, drift monitoring) for one WBM decision-support claim, reviewable by a notified body.
8. **Cell-type-appropriate rare-disease models at scale.** GD work was constrained by fibroblast-only material. *Success:* automated construction of disease-relevant cell-type models for ≥10 lysosomal/mitochondrial disorders whose predictions match independent exometabolomics better than generic Recon3D.
