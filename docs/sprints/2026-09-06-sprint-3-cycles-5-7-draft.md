**Fifth cycle: what the gap-filler taught the protocol.** Tracing the 42 carbon-source conditions on which the drafts' wild types fail (`scripts/condition_gapfill.py`: for each, the cheapest additions from the universe next to the genes whose fitness is specific to that condition) exposed three artefacts in a row, each caught only because the verifier now has physics as well as fitness data. First, for nine P. putida conditions the cheapest fill was a reversible IspG variant plus a formate lyase, giving growth rates of 3.7 per hour: the reversible reaction, next to its NADH-dependent twin, turns NADH into reduced ferredoxin and from there into NADPH, quinol and NADH without limit — an energy-generating cycle that the closed-exchange test (`gembench/checks.py`) shows at 500 mmol/gDW/h. The universe reaction is now irreversible (universe_patches_v0.1, MECDPDH4E) and the gap-filler tests every candidate set for energy cycles and cuts off the offending ones before it accepts a fill. Second, the ATP synthase of §4 — the cheapest fill for propionate and isoleucine in S. oneidensis and for stachyose in B. thetaiotaomicron was the ATP synthase itself, which is how the omission was found. Third, once the drafts had an ATP synthase, every latent proton-bookkeeping error paid out as growth: unlimited Fe(III) as an electron acceptor for a gene-less ferric reductase in B. thetaiotaomicron (0.61 per hour anaerobically), a reversible medium-completion carrier that let a sodium-coupled alanine symporter run as a free cycle in S. meliloti (250 mmol ATP/gDW/h from nothing), and free bicarbonate uptake as a cytoplasmic proton sink (114 mmol ATP/gDW/h). These are decision D17 (§6), and every arm of the benchmark was re-computed under it; the numbers in §3 and in the table above are the re-computed ones (the changes were within ⟨x⟩ MCC everywhere except ⟨...⟩).

**Sixth cycle: conditions, with genes.** With the guards in place the condition fills are informative. The gap-filler's proposals are parsimonious, not biological — it offers PTS transporters to Bacteroides and rhizobia, which have none, and CoA-transferases where the annotation has a CoA ligase — so each proposal was checked against the genome-wide feature table and the condition-specific fitness genes, and adopted only as an annotated reaction with its genes (`model_patches_v0.3.json`, 19 additions; `gpr_patches_v0.4.json`, nine rule corrections). What the fitness data named was, in every case, a pathway the draft had misassigned or never assigned: S. meliloti has no succinate dehydrogenase in the draft although sdhCDAB is in the genome (the cheapest fill for succinate, histidine, glutamine and proline was that one reaction) and its N-acetylglucosamine cluster SMc02869–SMc02877 is an ABC transporter, a kinase and a SIS-domain deaminase that the draft had put on the anabolic glucosamine-6-phosphate synthase; S. oneidensis has the same nagB mis-assignment (nagB-II on GF6PTA, no deaminase) and its NupC transporter carries every nucleoside but inosine; P. putida's uronate dehydrogenase (PP_1171) was on dTDP-glucose 4,6-dehydratase, its hexuronate TRAP transporter on a succinate ABC system, and the liu pathway between the branched-chain keto-acid dehydrogenase and HMG-CoA lyase was missing entirely; B. thetaiotaomicron's fructose transporter (BT1758, in the fructan utilisation locus) and a D-ribulokinase for the D-arabinose route were absent. ⟨results: conditions rescued per organism, gene-level changes and how many confirmed, MCC⟩. The conditions that remain unexplained are transporters without a gene the data can name (mannose, trehalose, melibiose, glucosamine and the maltooligosaccharides of B. thetaiotaomicron, whose SusC/SusD systems the universe cannot represent; mannose, glucosamine, uridine and tagatose in S. meliloti), one pathway the universe lacks (the oxidative L-arabinose pathway of rhizobia, which the fitness data spell out gene by gene: SM_b20890 araD, SM_b20891, SM_b20892, the ChvE/MmsB transporter), and the valine and isoleucine degradation branches of P. putida.

**Seventh cycle: the reference gap-fill and the biomass template, revisited.** The eleven gene-less reactions that the reference gap-fill had added (D14) were each traced to the biomass precursor they serve. Four turned out to serve a precursor the organism does not make: the CarveMe universal biomass demands menaquinol, but P. putida and S. meliloti are ubiquinone organisms with no menaquinone pathway in their genomes, and to satisfy the demand the gap-filler had invented a route to the naphthoquinone ring through oxalyl-CoA decarboxylase and a phenylpyruvate oxidation. Removing menaquinol from those two biomass equations (`model_patches_v0.4.json`) removes the need for the P. putida fill altogether — the shipped P. putida draft fails to grow on its reference condition only because of the template. The S. oneidensis fills were workarounds for three annotated-but-missing steps, now added with their genes: the acetylornithine deacetylase argE (the draft had made ornithine by running an ornithine cyclodeaminase backwards from proline), the quinone-linked L-lactate dehydrogenase LldEFG (the fill was an NAD-dependent LDH without a gene, on the organism's own reference substrate), and the znuABC zinc transporter (the fill took zinc in through a citrate/acetate antiport). The B. thetaiotaomicron and S. meliloti fills were right reactions without genes and now carry them (nadB, the two-subunit NAD-dependent dihydroorotate dehydrogenase, pdxH, the acetylornithine carbamoyltransferase argF' as a lumping, dxs). ⟨results⟩.
